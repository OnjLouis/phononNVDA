Phonon for NVDA

Offline speech using Gradium's multilingual Phonon engine. Includes 102 voices for English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese. No Ollama server, GPU, separate synthesizer add-on or first-use download is required.

Requirements and Setup

Requires 64-bit NVDA 2026.2 or later, Windows 10 or later and an AVX2-capable processor. Older processors and 32-bit NVDA cannot load this runtime.

Install the add-on and restart NVDA. Open the synthesizer selection dialog with NVDA+Control+S and choose Phonon. Alternatively, open NVDA Settings, Phonon and choose Use Phonon. The model and voices are included in the package.

Phonon and Ollama Speech are separate add-ons and can coexist. Installing this add-on does not change your Ollama Speech connection or settings.

Speech Controls

Choose Language filter and Voice in Speech settings or the synth settings ring. English Character Voices groups the catalogue's character voices separately from British and American English. The filter limits the voice list; it does not translate text. The model supports the five languages listed above.

Rate, Rate boost, Pitch and Volume use NVDA's streaming Sonic processing. Audio plays through NVDA's selected output device. Shift pauses or resumes; Control stops speech. The engine finishes its current calculation before starting replacement speech, so responsiveness depends on the processor.

If the native engine fails, the active driver switches to eSpeak NG to preserve access. Keep a familiar alternative synthesizer available while testing.

Settings and Updates

The Phonon settings panel contains Use Phonon, Check for updates, Check for updates now and Help. Update intervals are Never, Hourly or Daily; Daily is the default. Help opens this manual. No Tools-menu shortcut is added.

Updates require a valid Phonon signature and matching package identity, version, size and hash. Downloads are staged outside the add-ons folder and installed by NVDA after your approval. Restart NVDA when prompted. Saved speech settings are kept.

Privacy

Speech is generated on this computer. Spoken text and audio are not saved or sent to a server. Only update checks and approved update downloads use the network. Automatic update checks can be disabled.
